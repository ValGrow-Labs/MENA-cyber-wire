> **"Regional Cyber Intelligence. Real-time Awareness."**  
> *"Know the Threat. Understand the Impact."*

## 🚀 Key Product Features

### 1. Threat-to-Action Protocol
Every verified intelligence piece answers:
- **IMPACT**: Why it matters & potential blast radius
- **ACTION**: Defensive steps for IT teams and users
- **AFFECTED**: Target scope across regional verticals
- **Technical Remediation Checklist**: Actionable mitigation checklist

### 2. Signal vs. Noise Classification
- **SIGNAL**: Corroborated, verified intelligence with confirmed technical indicators.
- **WATCH**: Developing threats actively monitored by regional analysts.
- **UNVERIFIED**: Claims, leak claims, or unconfirmed rumors under verification.

### 3. Interactive MENA Cyber Impact Map
Interactive visualization and dossiers for **13 nations**:
🇦🇪 UAE &middot; 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia &middot; 🇶🇦 Qatar &middot; 🇪🇬 Egypt &middot; 🇧🇭 Bahrain &middot; 🇰🇼 Kuwait &middot; 🇴🇲 Oman &middot; 🇯🇴 Jordan &middot; 🇱🇧 Lebanon &middot; 🇮🇶 Iraq &middot; 🇲🇦 Morocco &middot; 🇹🇳 Tunisia &middot; 🇩🇿 Algeria

### 4. Regional Cyber Pulse
Light-colored telemetry panel tracking DEFCON posture, targeted critical infrastructure sectors, and active threat actor campaigns (MuddyWater, OilRig, LockBit, DarkBit).

### 5. Social Editorial Feed (Reddit + Instagram Style)
- **Feed Tabs**: Latest Feed &middot; Most Discussed &middot; Trending &middot; My Following
- **Follow Countries**: Users can follow specific countries with real-time feed filtering.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Upvoting, local bookmarks, instant clipboard sharing, threaded comments, and card vs. compact layout toggles.

### 6. Critical CVE Watch & Government Advisories
Direct integration with the National Vulnerability Database (NVD API 2.0) and CISA alerts, color-coded by CVSS severity in Coastal Tech tones.
